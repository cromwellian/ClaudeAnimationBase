# The Butterfly (15 s)

Logline: Clawd wants to catch a butterfly, but every grab misses and ends in a tumble, so Clawd gives up, and the butterfly lands on Clawd of its own accord.

World: one sunny meadow. Pale morning blue sky, green grass, rose and cream flowers. Colour arc: cool bright morning (A) → warmer mid-day chase (B) → golden late-afternoon light (C) as Clawd's mood goes from grabby to still.

Motif: **the flower.** In A the butterfly lands on a tall flower; Clawd pounces, crushes it, and comes up wearing the bloom on its head. Clawd wears it through the chase. In C the butterfly lands on that same bloom: Clawd has become the flower.

Clawd's arc: happy (humming) → surprised → starstruck → mischief (sneaking) → determined (pounce) → confused (flower on head) → determined (chase) → excited (the big leap) → dizzy (crash) → sad (gave up) → surprised (it landed on me) → love.

Screen direction: the butterfly and the chase always travel right. In C the butterfly returns from upper right.
